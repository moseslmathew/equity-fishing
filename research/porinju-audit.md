## Biography and approach for profile

Bio: Kochi-based value investor and founder of Equity Intelligence, following an earlier career at Kotak Securities and Parag Parikh Financial Services.

Core idea: Buy overlooked businesses below their underlying value and allow business progress to close that gap.

Detail: His firm's published framework combines bottom-up fundamental research with market intelligence, business-owner thinking and a margin of safety. It favours independent judgement about neglected opportunities and patient ownership, while adapting when business facts change. This is a summary of the firm's stated approach, not a claim that every personal holding follows a verified checklist.

Focus: (1) Price below conservatively assessed business value; (2) Bottom-up business and balance-sheet research; (3) Patience with neglected opportunities, with ongoing reassessment.

Quarterly named disclosure thresholds hide many smaller positions. Provider values can lag. Report dates and legal holders accompany all published entries.
