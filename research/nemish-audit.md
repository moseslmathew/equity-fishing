### LMW

Provider rows distinguish 559,673 shares / 5.24% from 354,200 shares / 3.32%. Trendlyne's holder history explicitly names the latter amount Nemish S Shah Huf in December 2023 and earlier quarters; its current display omits that suffix. This historical exact-quantity continuity, rather than the difference in categories alone, is the reason not to add it to personal ownership. Rekha N Shah's 1.47% is a separate holder. Finology's 10.03% is not used for the personal percentage. No assertion that the household's combined ownership is wrong.

### Asahi

Block.deal and Rediff show 3,363,183 shares / 1.32% and 11,119,635 / 4.36%, both displayed with the same name. The first is in the resident-individual category. Stocks Sena selects 1.32%; Solomo investor selects 4.36%; Finology shows 5.68%. BFSL's indexed June text puts the larger amount under HUF, but the full BFSL page timed out and the original NSE link returned an internal retrieval error. A category/name difference alone does not conclusively establish a distinct legal person, so the dataset withholds the total personal percentage and quantity. Source details retain both observations. Do not silently treat 5.68% or 1.32% as definitively reconciled personal ownership.

### Exclusions

Finology investor page includes Investment & Precision Castings at 10.00% and Vascon Engineers at 3.79%. Its own company pages name those shares to Rekha N Shah; Vascon is additionally corroborated by Trendlyne, Rediff and Block.deal. These companies are excluded from Nemish's personal list, without claiming the family has exited. Old Hi-Tech Gears, Popular Foundations, EID Parry, Super Spinning Mills and JSW entries are not included as current; Stocks Sena lists them as past disclosures. Missing disclosure is not proof of zero ownership.

## Profile text for UI (paraphrases)

Biography: Nemish Shah co-founded ENAM and is associated with its long-term investment tradition. ENAM's official team page identifies him as a commerce graduate and philanthropist; FLAME University identifies him as an ENAM co-founder.

Core idea: Buy deeply understood businesses at prices that offer substantial upside with protection against permanent loss, then give the investment time.

Detail: His ENAM colleague Manish Chokhani's 2004 interview describes an emphasis on mispriced opportunities with a large potential payoff and research into alternative business outcomes. ENAM's current published approach combines long ownership, bottom-up company research, management capital-allocation quality and margin of safety. The firm's current framework informs this summary; it is not a newly published personal rulebook from Shah.

Focus: (1) Business economics and operating leverage; (2) Management quality and capital allocation; (3) Valuation, downside and size of potential payoff.

## Reliability limits

Provider corroboration is agreement across domains, not proof that their data feeds or interpretation are independent. Category suffixes can be lost by parsers. An exchange record describes one reporting date, and public disclosure thresholds conceal other positions. No rankings based on guaranteed performance or unsupported claim of being an early-stage-only investor. Records remain provider status; the unresolved Asahi record is review status.
